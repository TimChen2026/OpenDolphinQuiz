## Overview

The Team tab configures your sales team structure and responsibilities — the foundation of email routing and project ownership.

<img src="/docs/team.png" alt="Team interface" />

## Sales Director

- Enter the sales director's email as the **CC** recipient for internal notification and warning emails
- Saved immediately

## Sales Managers

- **Add a manager**: enter a member's email to add them as a sales manager
- **Remove a manager**: they stop receiving inquiry and warning emails
- Edit each manager's **phone number** for internal contact

## Assigning Themes

- Each sales manager can be assigned **multiple themes** (e.g. "Kids English", "Adult IELTS")
- When a prospect picks a theme in the quiz, the **internal notification** and **yellow/red warning** emails go to the manager responsible for that theme
- Projects without an assigned theme default to the team admin
