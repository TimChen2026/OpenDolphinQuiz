## Overview

The Analytics module visualizes business data through **10 ECharts charts**, covering prospect traffic behavior and sales team performance.

## Chart List

| # | Chart | Metric | Plan |
|---|-------|--------|------|
| 1 | Weekly visits | Past week, by weekday | Free |
| 2 | Visit timezone distribution | Past week, in 2-hour buckets | Pro+ |
| 3 | Monthly visits | Past 13 months, by month | Pro+ |
| 4 | Quarterly visits | Q1–Q4 stats | Pro+ |
| 5 | Yearly visits | By year | Pro+ |
| 6 | Theme visits | Past month, by theme | Pro+ |
| 7 | Theme × timezone visits | 12 buckets × 4 themes | Pro+ |
| 8 | Projects handled by manager | Manager × status (Won/Lost/Follow-up) | Pro+ |
| 9 | Themes handled by manager | Manager × theme | Pro+ |
| 10 | Avg. response time by manager | Manager avg. reply hours | Pro+ |

## Usage Notes

- Chart 1 is **visible for free**; charts 2–10 require **Pro / Max** (locked in the MVP)
- Hover charts for values; they adapt automatically on window resize
- Data updates daily; the latest inquiries and follow-ups are reflected in the stats
