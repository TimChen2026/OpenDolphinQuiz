## Prospects Can't Open the Quiz Link

1. Make sure the link was generated **after the check passed** (missing theme keywords can cause issues)
2. In **Templates & Links**, click "Re-check", fix the listed issues, and regenerate the link
3. Confirm the quiz is saved and your plan still has quiz quota
4. Try another browser or a mobile browser (some environments block links)

## Internal Notification Email Not Received

1. In **Report Templates**, confirm the "Internal Notification" template targets the sales manager of the theme and CCs the director
2. In **Team**, confirm the manager's email is correct and the manager is assigned the **responsible theme**
3. Check the **spam folder**
4. If everything looks right, ask an admin to check the email sending service configuration

## Warning Emails Not Triggering

1. In **Email Settings**, confirm the yellow/red thresholds match expectations (default 24h / 48h)
2. Confirm the project is in **Follow-up** status and unresponded beyond the threshold
3. Confirm recipients (responsible manager) and CC (director) are configured

## Analytics Charts Show No Data

1. Charts 2–10 require **Pro / Max**; the Free plan only shows chart 1
2. Confirm prospects have actually completed the quiz (traffic data comes from real visits)
3. Data is aggregated by period and updates daily; check back later
4. Charts resize automatically; refresh the page if rendering looks off

## Quiz Style Not Applied

1. In **Interaction**, select a style and click **Confirm** (selecting alone doesn't save)
2. Regenerate the quiz link (the link carries the style parameter)
3. Ask prospects to **refresh** the quiz page before answering

## Project Data Not Refreshing

1. In **Database → Project Data**, refresh manually or adjust the auto-refresh interval
2. Confirm the browser network is fine and retry shortly

## Login or Email Verification Issues

1. After signing up, click the **verification link** in your email
2. If the email doesn't arrive, check spam or resend
3. Google login requires entering your **team/organization name** on first use

## Inquiry Quota Reached

- An upgrade banner appears at the top of the project board. When the quota is exhausted, upgrade your plan or wait for the next period — see [Quickstart](./quickstart) for plan details.

## Still Stuck?

Contact support at `huiting.chen@outlook.com` and provide:

- A description of the issue and steps to reproduce
- Relevant screenshots
- The time of the error and the project number involved
