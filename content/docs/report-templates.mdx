## Template Types

The Report Templates module manages 4 email templates. Each shows its **To** and **CC** recipients so you can verify the mail flow at a glance.

| Template | Trigger | To | CC |
|----------|---------|----|----|
| Internal Notification | New inquiry submitted | Sales manager of the theme | Sales director |
| Summary | After prospect completes quiz (shown on result page, not emailed) | Prospect | — |
| Yellow Warning | Project unresponded for ≥ 24h | Sales manager | Sales director |
| Red Warning | Project unresponded for ≥ 48h | Sales manager | Sales director |

## Editing Templates

For each template you can edit the **subject** and **body**. The body supports `@variable` placeholders that are replaced with real data when sent.

Available variables (names are fixed Chinese strings; they are replaced with real data when sent):

- `@项目编号` — project number (customer-date-time)
- `@客户名` — customer name
- `@客户邮箱` — customer email
- `@客户电话` — customer phone
- `@主题` — the theme the prospect chose
- `@选择路径` — the prospect's full selection path
- `@询盘时间` / `@持续时间` — inquiry time and unresponded duration
- `@销售经理` — the responsible sales manager
- `@今日询盘次数` / `@定价页链接` — inquiry quota and upgrade link

Click **Save** to apply immediately; future emails use the new template.

## Notes

- The Summary template content is what the prospect sees on the **result page** after completing the quiz
- The "Inquiry near/at limit" reminder template is internal to the platform and only adjustable by admins
- After editing templates, regenerate the link in **Templates & Links** before sharing
