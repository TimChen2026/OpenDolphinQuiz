## How Warnings Work

When an inquiry project is **not responded to within the configured time**, the system automatically sends warning emails to remind the sales team and prevent lost prospects.

## Warning Thresholds

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Yellow warning threshold | ≥ 24 hours | Warns when a project is unresponded for this long |
| Red warning threshold | ≥ 48 hours | Escalates when a project is unresponded for this long |

<img src="/docs/email-settings.png" alt="Email settings" />

## How to Configure

1. Change the yellow/red thresholds (in hours)
2. Validation: yellow must be ≥ 1 hour; red must be greater than yellow
3. Click **Save** to write to the database — applied immediately

## Warning Email Content

The subject and body of warning emails are edited in **Report Templates** (yellow warning / red warning). They go to the responsible sales manager and CC the sales director.
