## 1. Sign Up & Log In

Visit the website homepage and click **Log in** or **Sign up** in the top-right corner:

- **Email + password** signup is supported; verify your email after registering
- **Google account** one-click login is supported (you'll be asked for your team/organization name on first login)

You'll land in the dashboard after logging in.

## 2. First-Time Setup

A new account automatically includes an **education reference quiz** (a complete P1–P4 decision tree), so you can try the full flow without building from scratch.

You can also load it anytime in **Templates & Links** by clicking "Use Reference Template".

## 3. Four Steps to Go Live

### Step 1: Edit your quiz

Go to **Interaction**, modify questions and options in the phone preview, fill in a **theme keyword** for each option, and pick a **style** (Oxford Navy, Princeton Orange, MIT Tech, and more). Click **Save** and **Confirm** to apply.

### Step 2: Generate a link

Go to **Templates & Links**, click "Check Quiz Info", and once it passes, click "Generate Link" to get the quiz URL. Copy and share it with prospects.

### Step 3: Prospect completes the quiz

The prospect answers P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 and confirms on the result page. The system creates a project number and sends an **internal notification email** to the responsible sales manager.

### Step 4: Follow up

Open the **Project Board**, review new inquiries, and move each project to **Won** or **Lost**.

## 4. Plans

| Plan | Description |
