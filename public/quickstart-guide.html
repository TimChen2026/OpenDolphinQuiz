Create and Configure Your Quiz

Sep 01 2026 · 20 steps

Create an account, configure quiz branching and appearance, manage team assignments and settings, preview the quiz, and open templates and sharing links.

To start the guide, navigate to:
Navigate to https://dolphinquiz.com/ to begin.
1

Open Sign Up

Select Sign Up to open the registration form and create an account.

2

Complete Registration

Enter the required details, select Sign Up, and verify your email to activate the account.

3

Open the Dashboard

Select Dashboard to access the quiz and project tools.

4

Open the Interaction Editor

Select Interaction to configure the quiz flow.

5

Continue Branch Setup

Select the highlighted item to continue configuring the active branch.

6

Set Option Display

Adjust the options according to your design to see if they are displayed.

7

Confirm the entire Quiz Flow

Adjust the logic of the options until they meet your design requirements.

8

Choose your preferred style

Select one style to apply.

9

Preview the Quiz

Select Preview to review the configured quiz before continuing.

10

Open Report Templates

In the initial stage, it is recommended not to make any modifications in Report Templates.

11

Confirm your team

Confirm sales manager and sales director

12

Add a Sales Manager

Select Add Sales Manager to continue setting up the sales team hierarchy.

13

Set Sales Director

Select Set Sales Director to apply the leadership assignment.

14

Assigned all themes to your team

Select one theme to add it to one sales manager

15

Save Phone Details

Select Save Phone to apply the updated phone information.

16

Email Settings

Confirm Warning Time Settings

17

Templates & Links

Confirm that there are no error prompts in the Check quiz information

18

Generate a Quiz Link

Create a shareable link.

19

Copy the Quiz Link

Copy the generated quiz link so it can be shared with your friends.

20

Watch the final effect

Click on the link obtained on your phone or browser to enter your first DolphinQuiz!
